Subscriptions

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Newsletter. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge.

I paid but the app doesn't show my plan.

Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Editions and content

A story looks wrong.

Content is written by AI from public news sources and can contain mistakes. Mark the story looks wrong in the reader and it will be re-checked. Every story links to its sources so you can check the original reporting.

My edition hasn't arrived.

Check the delivery schedule in the newsletter's settings and make sure notifications are allowed for Newsletter in iOS Settings. If it still doesn't arrive, email us.

Your account and data

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. Your data is deleted as described in the Privacy Policy. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel it in iOS Settings too.
