Newsletter — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

In short: Newsletter is an independent app made by an individual developer (Junming Chen). We do not collect your email address, do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools, and cannot access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health data — the app never asks for those permissions.

We do store your account identifier, your reading preferences, and content you type into the app (like questions you ask in the Reader) so we can generate and personalize your newsletters. Some of that content is sent to OpenAI to generate text, under a no-retention setting. We never sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Newsletter is developed and operated by an individual developer, Junming Chen ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what information the Newsletter iOS app and its backend services collect, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Contact: Jeremychen124@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account & sign-in

You sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, handled by Firebase Authentication. From this we receive and store:

  • A unique account identifier (not your email address — we do not read or store your email, even if Apple or Google make it available to us)
  • A display name (defaults to a generic name; you can change it in Settings)
  • An optional profile photo you choose to add from your photo library, resized and stripped of location/camera metadata (EXIF) before storage

2.2 Content preferences & personalization

To build and personalize your newsletters, we store:

  • Your reading preferences — topics, coverage rules, delivery schedule, explanation style, and any free-text requests you type (e.g., "focus more on X")
  • "Memory" items — short notes about your interests or context, either things you told the app directly or things it inferred from your usage, used to personalize future editions
  • Reading progress — how far you've read in each edition, kept for 14 days and used only to sync your place across your own devices
  • Questions you ask using the Reader's "Ask" feature, including any text you highlight for context, and the app's answers

2.3 Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device push token (used only to deliver a daily "your edition is ready" alert). Notification content never includes your newsletter's story text, personalization data, or credentials.

2.4 Subscription & payments

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase and managed by RevenueCat. We receive your subscription/plan status linked to your account — we never receive or store your card number, bank details, or other payment credentials; Apple and RevenueCat handle those directly.

2.5 What we do not collect

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are integrated in the app
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, health data, or calendar — the app never requests these permissions and cannot access them
  • No password, since sign-in is handled entirely by Apple/Google

3. How we use your information

  • To generate, schedule, and personalize your newsletter editions
  • To answer questions you ask in the Reader
  • To sync your reading progress and preferences across your own devices
  • To send you notifications you've opted into
  • To manage your subscription entitlement
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the service

4. On-device processing

Certain small pieces of text — like your home screen's daily greeting and card summaries — are generated entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Models. That data never leaves your device or reaches our servers.

6. Data retention

  • Reading progress is kept for 14 days, then deleted automatically
  • Account, preference, and memory data are kept until you delete your account
  • Notification device tokens are kept until you disable notifications, sign out, or delete your account

7. Your rights and choices

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app. This triggers a complete erasure process: it deletes your Firebase sign-in record and permanently removes your account data — preferences, memory items, reading progress, notification devices, and question history — from our systems.

California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose about you (see Section 2 above)
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app account deletion feature or email us at Jeremychen124@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity (e.g., by confirming your account) before completing a request.

Users outside the United States

Our services are operated from and primarily intended for users in the United States. If you access the app from outside the U.S., your data will be processed in the U.S. as described here. Contact us at the address above with any regional privacy questions.

8. Children's privacy

Newsletter is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including Firebase's authentication and access controls — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
