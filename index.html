Newsletter — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

In short: Newsletter is an independent app made by an individual developer (Junming Chen). We do not use analytics or advertising tools; the only diagnostic data collected is crash reports (see Crash reports below). We cannot access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health data — the app never asks for those permissions.

We store your account identifier, the display name your Apple or Google account provides, your reading preferences, and content you type into the app (like requests and questions you ask in the Reader) so we can generate and personalize your newsletters. Your sign-in provider's email address is held by our sign-in service, Firebase Authentication; our own database does not store it. Requests and questions are sent to OpenAI, an AI service, to generate text, under a no-retention setting. Newsletters are generated by AI and can contain mistakes. We never sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Newsletter is developed and operated by an individual developer, Junming Chen ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what information the Newsletter iOS app and its backend services collect, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Contact: Jeremychen124@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account & sign-in

You sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, handled by Firebase Authentication. From this we receive and store:

  • A unique account identifier and the sign-in provider (Apple or Google)
  • Your email address: the app asks Apple or Google for it at sign-in, and it is kept in your Firebase Authentication record (Apple may give a private relay address instead of your real one). Our own database does not store your email, and we do not use it to contact you or send it to any other provider.
  • A display name: the name your Apple or Google account provides (or a generic name if none is provided) is stored with your account, and you can change it in Settings.
  • An optional profile photo you add from your photo library, resized and stripped of location/camera metadata (EXIF) before storage. If you sign in with Google, the app may show your Google profile photo on your device; that photo is not uploaded to our servers unless you choose it as your photo.

2.2 Content preferences & personalization

To build and personalize your newsletters, we store:

  • Your reading preferences — topics, coverage rules, delivery schedule, explanation style, and any free-text requests you type (e.g., "focus more on X")
  • "Memory" items — short notes about your interests or context, either things you told the app directly or things it inferred from your usage, used to personalize future editions
  • Reading progress — how far you've read in each edition, kept for 14 days and used only to sync your place across your own devices
  • Which editions you have opened, and when the app was last open, so that a missed edition's stories can be carried into the next one and so that we can stop generating editions you are not reading: scheduled editions are paused for your whole account if the app has not been opened for 7 days, and a single newsletter is paused (and shows a Resume button) after 5 of its editions in a row were delivered but never opened. Opening the app resumes an account pause, and we then prepare today's edition right away
  • Reactions — when you mark a story as liked, not for me, or looks wrong (optionally with a short note), we keep the reaction and your note for 365 days, and use them to adjust your newsletter's learned preferences
  • Questions you ask using the Reader's "Ask" feature, including any text you highlight for context, and the app's answers
  • Your newsletters and the editions generated for you, which are kept with your account

2.3 Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device push token (used only to deliver a daily "your edition is ready" alert, and one short reminder when your newsletters are paused after 7 days without opening the app). Notification content never includes your newsletter's story text, personalization data, or credentials.

2.4 Subscription & payments

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase and managed by RevenueCat. We receive your subscription/plan status linked to your account — we never receive or store your card number, bank details, or other payment credentials; Apple and RevenueCat handle those directly.

2.5 Anonymous story reports

If you mark a story "looks wrong", we re-check that story and save an anonymous report to improve accuracy. The report contains the story's text and date, your chosen reason and the result of the check. It contains no account identifier and never your note, and it is not linked to you. These reports have no fixed expiry and are not removed when you delete your account.

2.6 What we do not collect

  • No analytics or advertising SDKs are integrated in the app; the only diagnostics collected are crash reports (section 2.7)
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, health data, or calendar — the app never requests these permissions and cannot access them
  • No password, since sign-in is handled entirely by Apple/Google
  • No advertising identifier, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites

2.7 Crash reports

The app uses Google's Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash and diagnostic data when the app crashes or hits a serious error: your device model, operating system version, app version and build, stack traces (technical records of what the app was doing), and a pseudonymous identifier (a one-way hash of your account identifier, never your name or email). Crash reports contain none of your newsletter text, questions or requests. We use them only to find and fix bugs. Crashlytics retains this data for 90 days, per Google's policy.

3. How we use your information

  • To generate, schedule, and personalize your newsletter editions, including looking up current news for your topics
  • To answer questions you ask in the Reader
  • To sync your reading progress and preferences across your own devices
  • To send you notifications you've opted into
  • To manage your subscription entitlement
  • To find and fix crashes and other bugs (crash reports, section 2.7)
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the service

4. On-device processing

Certain small pieces of text — like your home screen's daily greeting and card summaries — are generated entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Models. That data never leaves your device or reaches our servers.

5A. AI-generated content

Newsletters, summaries and answers are generated by AI (OpenAI models) from news sources and from the requests and preferences you provide. By continuing past the welcome page of the app you acknowledge that your requests are processed by an AI service as described here. AI can make mistakes: check important facts against the linked sources.

6. Data retention

  • Reading progress is kept for 14 days, then deleted automatically
  • Reactions and their notes are kept for 365 days, then deleted automatically
  • Account, preference, newsletter, edition and memory data are kept until you delete your account (or delete the newsletter or memory item)
  • Notification device tokens are kept until you disable notifications, sign out, or delete your account
  • Anonymous story reports (section 2.5) have no fixed expiry
  • Crash reports (section 2.7) are retained by Crashlytics for 90 days, then deleted
  • After you delete your account we keep only a one-way hashed identifier of your sign-in (so the deleted account is not re-linked) and a record that the deletion completed; neither contains your content

7. Your rights and choices

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app. Deletion first blocks access to your account, then an automated job (which runs every 15 minutes and retries if a step fails) deletes your Firebase sign-in record, which includes your email, and permanently removes your account data — newsletters and editions, preferences, memory items, reactions, reading progress, profile photo, notification devices, and question history — from our systems.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; otherwise Apple continues to bill you. RevenueCat may keep your purchase record under its own retention terms; email us if you want it removed.

California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose about you (see Section 2 above)
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app account deletion feature or email us at Jeremychen124@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity (e.g., by confirming your account) before completing a request.

Users outside the United States

Our services are operated from and primarily intended for users in the United States. If you access the app from outside the U.S., your data will be processed in the U.S. as described here. Contact us at the address above with any regional privacy questions.
